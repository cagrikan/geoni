Who Does AI Visibility Work — And How To Choose One

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · GEONI Team

Three kinds of provider sell AI visibility (GEO/AEO) work: established agencies, independent consultants and measurement tools. There is no single “best” name — this is not yet a standardised service line. The choice that holds up is made on method, not reputation.

Three provider types, three different jobs

Agencies. Established SEO and digital marketing agencies are experienced in technical foundations, content production and authority building — and most of AI visibility rests on exactly those. If you are hiring an agency, the question is not “do you do GEO?” but “how do you measure the result?”

Independent consultants. Cheaper and more focused, and often faster to start. The risk is that the method lives in one person's head and the reporting comes down to screenshots.

Measurement tools. They do not do the work; they show you what is actually happening: which questions you appear in, which engine drops you, which sources the answers cite. Keeping an independent measurement layer while an agency does the work is the only way to see whether you got what you paid for. Comparison of the tools themselves: AI visibility tracking tools.

Four questions to ask any provider

  1. How many engines do you measure? ChatGPT alone is not enough. The same brand can appear in Perplexity and be absent from ChatGPT; the engines disagree far more than people expect.
  2. How much does the answer move when you ask the same question twice? A provider who can answer this has actually measured. One who says “it doesn't move” has not.
  3. Do you report which sources the answers cite? Who the model quotes is the data that tells you what to do next.
  4. Did you check that AI crawlers can read my site? Bot protection, a WAF or JavaScript-only rendering is the most common cause of invisibility — and content edits will not fix it.

Three red flags

“We'll get you ranked first in ChatGPT.” AI answers have no ranking. You are in the answer or you are not. Anyone using this sentence does not know the field.

A guaranteed timeline. Answers move with model updates. Committing to a date is committing to something outside anyone's control.

A one-off report. A single measurement is noise. The signal is a repeated question set and its trend.

Should I replace my SEO agency?

Probably not. The foundations are shared — a crawlable page, a clear answer, being mentioned in trusted sources. The difference is the outcome: in SEO the top ten works, in an AI answer there is no second place. For most companies, keeping the current agency and adding an independent measurement layer is both cheaper and faster than switching.

Does location matter?

Almost not at all. Every part of this work runs remotely: checking crawler access, building the question set, querying the engines on a schedule, editing content. Search results will naturally surface providers clustered in the largest cities, but a face-to-face meeting does not change the quality of the measurement. Judge transparency instead of postcode.

Frequently asked questions

Who do you recommend?

We do not publish a single name. Without comparative measurement, a “best provider” list cannot be defended. Ask the four questions above to three providers; the clarity of the answers will rank them for you.

Measurement first, or service first?

Measurement first. If you do not know where you stand at the start, you cannot tell later whether the service worked.

Can I do any of this myself?

Yes, and the free parts have the highest return: confirm your robots.txt allows the AI crawlers, and write one page per question you want to win, answering it in the first forty to sixty words.
